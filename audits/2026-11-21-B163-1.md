Since the previous audit: 1 fixed (#69), 2 new (#70, #71), #71 fixed by Claude in the same step after the audit (see below), 15 still open (including #67, accepted exception).

**#69 fixed.** `.tabs` (`docs.css:432-441`) uses `overflow-x: auto`; its buttons do not shrink or wrap (`docs.css:444-445`). A 4px padding (`border-width-2 × 2`), cancelled by a negative margin, plus `scroll-padding-inline`, keeps the focus ring unclipped. A click or a key (arrows, Home, End) scrolls the selected tab into view without animation (`ComponentPage.tsx:40-43`, `:57`, `:89`). `.tabs` left the margin reset (`docs.css:256`): the gap between blocks stays 16px. Checked in the browser by Claude: page width equals the viewport at 375px and 768px (row 609px wide in 335px at 375px), the End key selects Changelog and scrolls it into view, desktop unchanged. WCAG 1.4.10 allows sideways scrolling inside one part of the page.

**Change after the audit (Design System Lead, 2026-11-21).** The row follows the approved Tabs design (B093): scrollbar hidden (`scrollbar-width: none`, `::-webkit-scrollbar`), no button, the selected tab brought into view. No new element, token or value; checked in the browser by Claude (375px: row 608px in 335px, End selects Changelog and scrolls to it, page width = viewport).

**#71 fixed after the audit.** The row line was drawn on the padding box, so it ran 4px past the content column on each side. It is now drawn on the content box (`background … content-box`): checked in the browser, its left edge matches the page title and the selected underline covers it.
